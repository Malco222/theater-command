# Music licence log

One line a source. Ruling 144: public domain or CC0, or a licence a game that may be sold can honour,
credits kept in a list that ships with the game.

| What | Source | Licence | Credit owed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every instrument recording in the four pieces | VS Chamber Orchestra 2: Community Edition, github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (master, including its VSCO 1 percussion) | CC0 1.0; the readme adds "use these samples for ANY purpose", asks that the samples not be sold as samples, and asks for credit | Requested, not required: "Orchestral samples: VS Chamber Orchestra 2: Community Edition by Versilian Studios (Sam Gossner) and Ivy Audio (Simon Dalzell)". Add it to the game's credits. |
| The music itself (melodies, harmony, arrangement) | Written for this game in this thread | The project's own | None |
| The hall echo | Built by the program (`engine.make_hall`), no recorded room | The project's own | None |

Nothing is taken from any film, series, game, anthem or march. The tunes are built on the game's own
five-note call (F, B flat, C, A, D) from the sound team's brief.
