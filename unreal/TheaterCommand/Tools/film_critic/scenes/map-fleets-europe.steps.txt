# Film critic scene map-fleets-europe (and map-fleets-europe-heading, the same steps with -TheaterFleetHeading):
# the campaign map's fleets sailing north, south, west and east, each caught mid-sail, just arrived, and at rest a
# minute later (Brian 26 Nov: the wakes of fleets at rest all point screen-left).
# Britain, September 1939, seed 1. On this head (node, the rules bundle at 1939-09 with the release 2 switches):
#   Home Fleet in the North Sea, Western Approaches Fleet in the Western Approaches, Dover Fleet in the English
#   Channel, Mediterranean Fleet in the Eastern Mediterranean, all at sea (none in harbour). Every order below is
#   accepted (TC.forceOrder "fleet:<id>:<sea>:patrol", the string OrderFleetTo sends), the Mediterranean Fleet's
#   second order of the month too (a fleet's reach is counted from where it began the month).
# How the map sails a fleet (CampaignMapActor.cpp, ArmyPiece.cpp): by water, for T = 1.1 + length/380 s, held
# between 1.4 and 3.6 s, eased in and out; from 84% of the way the bow turns to lie as fleets at rest lie.
# The order-sea picture is taken 1.1 s after the order, so each leg below is long enough (T 2.1 to 2.5 s by the
# straight line, more by water) that the still is at 44 to 56% of the way, well before that turn:
#   north  Home Fleet to the Norwegian Sea          T 2.14  half way at about 1.15 s near Bergen
#   south  Western Approaches Fleet to the Iberian Coast  T 2.49  near Galicia
#   west   Mediterranean Fleet to the Central Mediterranean  T 2.25  between Crete and Derna
#   east   Mediterranean Fleet back to the Eastern Mediterranean  T 2.25  the same water, the other way
# A look-at waits for the place's fine ground, at least 3.5 s: a look-at on the destination right after an order is
# the "just arrived" still (about 5 s after the order); the second look-at there, a minute on, the "at rest" still.
# Film strips (-FilmCriticStrip=12,4): 4 s after each order-sea, the whole sail.
# The step player cannot turn the camera: every still here is at the table's own heading. The photographer scene
# photo-fleets-yaw takes the fleets at rest from four headings.
# No end-month here (the -heading copy is kept short).
# Each step's picture is step-NNN.png, NNN the step's number below.
