# video camera is super choppy ... it flickers and isn't smooth").
# France, January 1940, seed 1. DAY BOMBING: RHINELAND with Groupement de Chasse 3 and the bombers (groups 33 and 10),
# 250 fighters and 100 bombers against 450 fighters, FLY IT YOURSELF.
# Checked on the release 2 head 2fdb13e5 (node, the rules bundle Content/Rules/campaign-rules.js): France is offered
# in January 1940; TC.page("plan:bomb:7:33.10") is titled "DAY BOMBING: RHINELAND" and carries the fly offer;
# TC.airLook gives "The air battle over Rhineland"; the HUD's button reads "FLY IT YOURSELF" (CampaignHUD.cpp).

# 393, it is over at about 453. Its clock (AirDesk.cpp Clock::Step): x1 is half a battle second a real second, x4 two;
# x10 by itself until the formations meet and once the fight winds down; the opening flyover at half a second a second.
# Flags this scene needs: -TheaterAirCampaignLive (FLY IT YOURSELF is offered only with it in a picture run).
# Film strips (-FilmCriticStrip=12,24): every air-wait is filmed, at most 24 s each, and the press until the next strip.
# A check-province during the battle is a silent wait of about 66 s (45 s for the busy table, 1.1 s, 20 s more):
# the battle runs on, one still at its end, no strip. Used to pass the long dull stretches without filling the strip
# budget (1500 frames a run). No question card comes in an air battle unless a button of his asks it.
# Each step's picture is step-NNN.png, NNN the step's number below.
