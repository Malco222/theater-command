# Film critic scene land-desert-tobruk: a land battle fought live from the campaign (FIGHT IT YOURSELF), in the desert look.
# Britain, January 1941, seed 1: 7th Armoured Division attacks Tobruk (desert, held by Italy).
# Checked on the release 2 head 2fdb13e5 (node, the rules bundle with the release 2 switches): the names are found
# (TC.find), the army can attack the province, TC.battleLook gives the ticket "The Battle of Tobruk"; fought in the
# land rules (Content/Rules/land-rules.js, as LandLive does) it meets at about battle s 59 and is over at about
# 215. The field's look by LandScape::Pick's own boxes: desert (31.95 N, 23.9 E).
# -TheaterFightEveryLook offers FIGHT IT YOURSELF on every look (fight-yourself-looks.txt passes only some).
# The battle's keys (LandBattleScreen.cpp): Enter begins; 1/2/3 are x1/x2/x4 on his high view and x0.5/x1/x2 in the
# film; Tab picks his next unit; V rides with it; C is the film (and C or Esc takes the map back from the film).
# No order key (G F X K T B H R L) is pressed. A battle second is half a real second at x1.
# A land-wait waits at most 15 minutes; "begun" is waited only after Enter (in the placing view it never ends).
# Film strips (-FilmCriticStrip=10,12): each land-wait and the press, at most 12 s each.
# Each step's picture is step-NNN.png, NNN the step's number below.

# 001: the campaign table as it opens (UK, 1941-01)
start	UK 1941-01 seed 1
# ---- The battle of Tobruk: FIGHT IT YOURSELF, fought live (rules sim on this head: contact about 59 s, over about 215 s; look desert)
# 002: 7th Armoured Division picked up on the map: the army piece in hand, the provinces it can reach lit
select-unit	7th Armoured Division
# 003: the attack card for Tobruk: the odds, SEND THEM IN and FIGHT IT YOURSELF
order-province	Tobruk	card
# 004: the land battle opens (a second in: the field being laid, or its first frame)
press	FIGHT IT YOURSELF	does
# 005: 8 real s of the placing view: the field, its ground and both sides placed, before the battle begins
land-wait	8s
# 006: Enter (BEGIN): the battle begins, the high view
land-key	enter
# 007: pace x4 (2 battle s a real s) until contact; the high view
land-key	3
# 008: past any night before: the attack under way, seen from his high view
land-wait	begun
# 009: Tab: his next unit in hand, the view glides to it
land-key	tab
# 010: V: the film rides with that unit: a close view of its men or tanks
land-key	v
# 011: 12 real s riding with them toward the enemy at x4: men running, vehicles moving
land-wait	12s
# 012: until the first contact (about battle s 59), still riding with the unit
land-wait	contact
# 013: in the film the keys 1-3 are x0.5/x1/x2: x1 (half a battle s a real s) for the close fight
land-key	2
# 014: 16 real s of the close fight at x1: fire, men going to ground or falling
land-wait	16s
# 015: C: the film gives the camera back: his high view of the fight
land-key	c
# 016: Q held a second: his view turns (the view's own keys, no order)
land-key	q	view
# 017: W held a second: his view walks forward, lower over the field
land-key	w	view
# 018: C: the film takes the camera, choosing its own shots
land-key	c
# 019: 16 real s of the film's own cutting: each cut clean, the camera smooth between cuts
land-wait	16s
# 020: Esc: the map (his high view) back
land-key	esc
# 021: x4 to the end of the battle
land-key	3
# 022: the after card: who won, the losses, CONTINUE
land-wait	card	card
# 023: CONTINUE: the battle handed back to the campaign map
land-click	continue
# 024: the campaign map after the battle of Tobruk
check-province	Tobruk
