# Film critic scene land-poland-1939: a land battle fought live from the campaign (FIGHT IT YOURSELF), in the East, summer (farmland) look.
# Germany, September 1939, seed 1: XIX Corps attacks Pomorze (plains, held by Poland).
# Checked on the release 2 head 2fdb13e5 (node, the rules bundle with the release 2 switches): the names are found
# (TC.find), the army can attack the province, TC.battleLook gives the ticket "The Battle of Pomorze"; fought in the
# land rules (Content/Rules/land-rules.js, as LandLive does) it meets at about battle s 60 and is over at about
# 139. The field's look by LandScape::Pick's own boxes: farmland (53.3 N, 18.1 E).
# -TheaterFightEveryLook offers FIGHT IT YOURSELF on every look (fight-yourself-looks.txt passes only some).
# The battle's keys (LandBattleScreen.cpp): Enter begins; 1/2/3 are x1/x2/x4 on his high view and x0.5/x1/x2 in the
# film; Tab picks his next unit; V rides with it; C is the film (and C or Esc takes the map back from the film).
# No order key (G F X K T B H R L) is pressed. A battle second is half a real second at x1.
# A land-wait waits at most 15 minutes; "begun" is waited only after Enter (in the placing view it never ends).
# No film strips in this scene (the night's disk): the facts file still has the camera of every frame.
# Each step's picture is step-NNN.png, NNN the step's number below.
# Then a second battle the same month: I Corps (with Corps Wodrig, in Masuria) attacks Warsaw (urban, held by Poland):
# ticket "The Battle of Warsaw", a city field (ground cells: 4430 buildings, 2536 suburb, industry, a river,
# bridges); in the rules it meets at about 114 and is over at about 305. Whether the second ticket still stands
# after the first battle is fought live was not run in node (it is another army in another province).
