  when.hour        the local solar hour of the action (0 to 24)
  light            {sun {up, from}, moon {lit, up, from}, vis}: the sun's height and bearing, the moon's share lit,
                   height and bearing (degrees; bearings clockwise from north), how far a ship is seen (metres)
  sky              {cover 0-1, kind cumulus|deck|squalls, base, top (metres), rain 0-1, word}, dealt from the
                   campaign's weather.ts zone sky the same way air's sky.place.cloud is
  sea              {swell {height (m), period (s), from (deg)}, fetch (km)}
