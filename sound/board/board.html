Every sound made for the game, and seven scenes that show how they play together. Nobody who built them can hear, so the last check is yours. Press play, then stamp each one Good, Fix or Cut. Your stamps save by themselves. Listen once on headphones and once on the laptop's own speakers, which is how most players will hear it.
